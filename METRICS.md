# METRICS.md

## North Star Metric
**Total Aggregate Annual Savings Identified.**
We measure success not just by users, but by the "Impact" we find. If we find $1M in savings for our users, it means we've successfully demonstrated the value of the tool and generated massive goodwill for the Credex brand.

## Primary Conversion Funnel
1. **Landing Page Visitor → Step 1 Completed**: Target > 40%.
2. **Audit Completed → Lead Captured (Email)**: Target > 30%.
3. **Lead → Clicked "Talk to Credex"**: Target > 5% (Qualitative high-intent leads).

## Virality Metric (K-Factor)
**Shares per Audit.**
Target: 0.15. For every 100 audits, we want 15 users to share their public URL with a colleague or on social media. We track this via clicks on the "Share Link" button on the results page.

## Feedback Loop
1. **The "Surprise" Moment**: We track how often users change their "Current Spend" after seeing the "Recommended Spend." If they change it to be more accurate, it means they are engaging with the math.
2. **NPS (Post-Lead)**: After sending the PDF report, we ask one question: "On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend this audit to another founder?"
3. **Credex Lead Quality**: We sync with the Credex sales team weekly. If leads from the audit have a >20% higher close rate than cold outreach, the tool is a success.

## Technical Performance Metrics
- **Wizard Latency**: < 100ms for step transitions (Client-side).
- **Audit Execution**: < 1.5s total (including Claude API summary).
- **Lighthouse Performance Score**: > 90 (Crucial for mobile users coming from X/LinkedIn).
