# ECONOMICS.md

## Unit Economics of Lead Generation
CredX is a **Lead Magnet** for Credex (a service that sells discounted AI credits). We aren't selling the tool; we're selling the "Savings Opportunity."

### Converted Lead Value (CLV)
For Credex, a "Qualified Lead" is a startup spending >$2,000/mo on AI. 
- If Credex can save them 20% ($400/mo) via bulk credits.
- Credex takes a 5% margin on the spend ($100/mo).
- Average customer lifetime: 24 months.
- **Gross Lead Value**: $2,400.

### CAC per Channel (Projected)
- **Organic Social (X/LinkedIn)**: $0 (Time-based).
- **SEO (Long-tail AI pricing keywords)**: $5 - $10 / lead.
- **Paid Ads (Google Search "reduce ai spend")**: $15 - $25 / lead.
- **Average Blended CAC**: ~$12.

### Conversion Funnel: Audit → Consult → Purchase
1. **Visitor → Audit**: 25% (High intent).
2. **Audit → Lead (Email)**: 35% (Incentivized by PDF report).
3. **Lead → Credex Consultation**: 10% (Targeting the high-spenders >$500/mo savings).
4. **Consultation → Purchase (Conversion)**: 20%.
- **Overall Conversion (Visitor to Customer)**: 0.17%.

## $1M ARR in 18 Months: The Math
To reach $1M in annual revenue, Credex needs a portfolio of spend under management.

1. **Target Portfolio**: $20M in aggregate AI spend.
2. **Credex Margin**: 5%.
3. **Revenue**: $1M.

### How CredX delivers this:
- Average audit spend: $3,000/mo ($36k/yr).
- Customers needed: ~550 ($20M / $36k).
- At a 0.17% overall conversion rate, we need: **323,000 unique visitors**.
- Over 18 months, that's **18,000 visitors per month**.
- Or **600 visitors per day**.

Given the viral nature of the Neo-Brutalist shareable URL and the high search volume for AI cost management, 600 visitors/day is an extremely attainable goal for a free, high-utility tool.

## Pivot Trigger
If the **Audit → Lead** conversion drops below 15%, we pivot the value prop from "Savings" to "Compliance & Security." Founders might not care about $100/mo, but they care if their engineers are using personal credit cards and leaked API keys. We would add a "Security Audit" component to the tool to recapture interest.
