# GTM.md (Go-To-Market Strategy)

## Exact Target User
Our primary target is the **Series A Startup CTO or Engineering Lead**. These are individuals managing a team of 10-50 developers. At this scale, the AI "bill" starts to become a noticeable line item ($1k - $5k / mo), but they are too busy with feature delivery to manually audit every engineer's subscription. They care about two things: productivity and cost-efficiency.

## What they Google
- "Cursor vs Copilot for teams"
- "Claude 3 Team plan pricing"
- "OpenAI vs Anthropic API costs 2026"
- "How to reduce startup AI spend"
- "Best AI coding assistant for small teams"

## Where they hang out
- **X (Twitter)**: Following AI engineers like Andrej Karpathy and Greg Brockman.
- **Y Combinator (Hacker News)**: Specifically the "Show HN" and "Ask HN" threads.
- **Indie Hackers & Slack/Discord Communities**: Specifically "BuildSpace," "Founder University," and niche engineering leadership Slacks.
- **Product Hunt**: Looking for the latest productivity hacks.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0)
To get the first 100 users without spending a dime:
1. **The "Value-Add" DM**: Search X for founders complaining about their AI bill. Send a direct link to their pre-populated audit if they mention their stack (e.g. "Saw you use Cursor + Claude, I ran a quick check and you might be overpaying by $200. Check it here: [Link]").
2. **Reddit (r/startups & r/programming)**: Post a "State of AI Pricing" breakdown using the data from our `PRICING_DATA.md` and link the tool as a free calculator.
3. **LinkedIn Engineering Hooks**: Write a post comparing the "Hidden Costs" of different AI IDEs. Tag 5-10 CTOs in the network and ask for their opinion on the "Spend per Dev" metric.
4. **Founder Slacks**: Share the tool in the #growth or #engineering channels of private founder Slacks as a "Cool free tool I built to save our team $2k."

## Unfair Channel: The "Shareable Audit"
Our unfair channel is the **Shareable URL**. When a CTO finds out they can save $5,000/year, their first instinct is to share it with their CEO or CFO to show they are being fiscally responsible. By making the share page public and "Neo-Brutalist" (eye-catching), we turn every successful audit into a viral referral loop. Unlike a boring PDF, a live, dynamic URL feels like a modern SaaS product, encouraging the recipient to "Run my own audit" via the CTA at the bottom.
